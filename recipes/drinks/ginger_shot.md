# Ginger Shot

The ginger shots are known to be a boost to the inmune system.
Take them mostly in winter for better health.

---

**Ingredients**

- _Ginger_ (60 g)
- _Apple juice_ (400/500 mL)
- _1/2 lemon juice_ — Can be changed to any other acidic source, like _orange_ or _vinegar_.
- _Turmeric_/_Curcuma_ (Optional)
- _Honey_ (Optional) — 1 tablespoon is enough, but adapt to taste.

---

**Steps**

1. Wash the ginger and optionally peel it.
2. Add all ingredients into a blender and blend together. Optionally, pass the juice through a sieve to remove all clumps.
3. Alternatively, grate the ginger and squish as much juice as possible. Then mix all the liquids together.
