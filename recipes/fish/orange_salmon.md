# Orange Salmon (_Salmon a la Naranja_)

Delicious and simple Spanish recipe to cook salmon or any other fish.

---

**Ingredients**

- _Garlic_ (2~4 cloves)
- _Salmon_ (1 filet per person) — Other fishes can be used too.
- _Orange juice_ (200 mL)
- _Rosemary_ — Or use your desired herbs.

---

**Steps**

1. Slice the _garlic_ and put it to fry at low-mid fire for :clock: 2 minutes.
2. Put the _fish_ (skin on the pan first) and cook it at mid-high fire.
3. Add the _orange juice_ together with the _herbs_. Lower the fire and let it cook until the juice is reduced.
