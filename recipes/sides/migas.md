# Migas

One very traditional Spanish dish is "migas", cooked out of stale bread.

---

**Ingredients**

- _Stale bread_
- _Garlic_ (3~6 cloves)
- _Chorizo_
- _Vegetables_ — _Onion_, _bell pepper_, _carrot_, ... Be yourself.
- _Egg_ (optional)
- _Smoked paprika powder_
- _Olive oil_

!!! Note
    Any vegetable can actually be added here.

---

**Steps**

1. Break the _bread_ in small cubes in bite-sizes. If the bread is too dry, water it a little bit and then put it in the microwave.
2. Put a decent amount of oil in a pan, then stir-fry in it the _garlic_ (chopped in thin slices), and all the _vegetables_.
3. Add the _chorizo_ and any other meat that you might include. Cook for :clock: 5~7 minutes.
4. Lower the heat to medium-low, then add the _bread_ and the _paprika powder_. Let it all cook for :clock: 10 minutes.
5. You can top the plate with a _fried egg_ on top.

!!! Tip
    This dish is a great combination together with other "tapas", like "Pimientos de padron" or "Patatas bravas".
