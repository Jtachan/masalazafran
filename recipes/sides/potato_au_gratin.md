# Potato au Gratin

Creamy, cheesy and flavorful potatoes, which are a great side-dish for meat or fish.
It can also be eaten as a main dish.

---

**Ingredients** (2~3 servings)

- _Potatoes_ (450 g)
- _Butter_ (20 g)
- _Onion_ (1 medium / 2 small)
- _Garlic_ (2 cloves)
- _Milk_ (240 mL)
- _Heavy cream_ (60 mL)
- _Shredded cheddar_ (90 g)
- _Parmesan_ (15 g)
- _Parsley_
- _Salt and pepper_

---

**Steps**

1. Cut the _potatoes_ in thin slices. Wash them to partially remove some starch, then lay them in a tray.
2. Chop finely the _onion_ and _garlic_ and put it to cook with the _butter_ at medium-high heat, until they are translucent.
3. Add the _milk_ and _cream_ and slowly bring to boil, together with the _cheddar_, _salt_ and _pepper_. Do not stop stirring to prevent clumps.
4. Preheat the oven at 180 °C. Pour the mixture over the _potatoes_ and grate the _Parmesan_ on top.
5. Cover the tray with foil and bake it for :clock: 1 hour until the potatoes are tender.
6. Remove the foil cover and bake it for :clock: 20 minutes.
7. Let it sit for :clock: 5 minutes at room temperature before serving.
