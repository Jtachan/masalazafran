# Koriander-Mint Chutney

Refreshing and tangy at the same time.

---

**Ingredients**

- _Fresh koriander_ (100 g) — Use also the stems, not only the leaves
- _Fresh mint_ (10 g)
- _Tomato_ (1)
- _Ginger_ (1 cm chunk)
- _Green chilli_ (1~2 to taste, Optional) — Just for a spicy touch
- _Tamarind_ (Optional) — For an extra sweet touch
- _Salt_ and _black salt_

---

**Steps**

1. Chop any ingredient into small-like pieces, for the blender to work better.
2. Put all the ingredients in the blender, with half a glass of water to help blending everything.
3. Blend until smooth, then taste and adapt the salt to your liking.
