# Lentejas

This plate is named after its main ingredient: the lentils.

---

**Ingredients**

- _Yellow lentils_ (250 g)
- _Bell pepper_
- _Onion_
- _Carrot_
- _Potato_
- _Olive oil_
- _Garlic cloves_ (2 ~ 4)
- _Tomato sauce_
- _Chorizo_
- _Smoked paprika powder_
- _Bay leaves_
- _Cayene pepper_ (2), optional

!!! Tip
    Use a pressure-cook as the pot where it will be cooked.

---

**Steps**

1. Chop in big pieces (1 cm side) the _potato_, the _carrot_ and the _pepper_. Chop the onion in 8 pieces.
2. Put all the previous ingredients and the _garlic_ cloves at medium power over the stove with some _olive oil_. Cook for :clock: 5 minutes.
3. Add the _lentils_ and all the other ingredients, as well as some _salt_ and _pepper_. Stab the _chorizo_ and add it too. Put water until everything is covered.
4. Cook for :clock: 30~45 minutes in pressure-cook.
